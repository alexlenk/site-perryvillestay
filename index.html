The house

Bright, uncluttered, European style

Lovingly restored in 2022, the house keeps its original character and follows a clean, minimalist European style – bright rooms, uncluttered spaces and simple Scandinavian furniture, just like a home in Germany. One bedroom with a queen bed, one bathroom, for up to two guests. The owners' private bedroom and the basement are locked and not part of the rental.

Our story

American charm, German hospitality

We are a German-American family. When we restored this Perryville house in 2022, we wanted it to feel like a home in Germany: calm, bright and simple, with everything you need and nothing you don't. Perry County has deep German roots – Saxon Lutheran immigrants settled Altenburg and Frohna in 1839 – so the house feels right at home here.

Whether you are here for a few weeks of work or to visit family, we hope the house becomes your home away from home.

The area

In the heart of Perryville

Perryville sits on I-55 between St. Louis and Memphis. Local employers, the regional airport and the area's heritage sites are all close by.

For work

  • Perryville's manufacturers and businesses
  • Perryville Regional Airport, 9 miles north of town – aircraft maintenance and private aviation
  • Cape Girardeau, about 30 minutes south

To visit

  • Missouri's National Veterans Memorial, opened in 2019
  • National Shrine of Our Lady of the Miraculous Medal
  • Saxon Lutheran Memorial in Frohna – a log-cabin village on the National Register
  • Historic Ste. Genevieve, about 30–40 minutes north
